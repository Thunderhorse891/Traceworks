Before you order

What if WCAD returns no exact match?

The report will document the search, what WCAD returned, and why the match could not be confirmed. It will not substitute unrelated web results.

Is this a title or lien report?

No. It does not establish legal ownership, clear title, deed history, or liens. Those require county-clerk or title-company research.
